About

Hi, I’m Reinaldo — a System Administrator who genuinely enjoys making technology work smoothly, securely, and reliably for the people who depend on it.

For the past several years, I’ve cared for networks, servers, and systems in healthcare environments — places where downtime isn’t just inconvenient, it matters. I’ve reduced security risks, improved system availability, and helped colleagues grow through knowledge sharing. I work with Linux and Windows, Python, Docker, FreeBSD (pfSense), and I value Agile approaches like SCRUM. But more than tools, I value the people behind the screens.

I’m also a theologian and have been a Christian since 2014. Studying Theology and the Bible has deeply shaped who I am — it taught me to listen carefully, to serve humbly, and to pursue truth with patience. It also reminds me that every system I touch, every problem I solve, and every person I help is part of something bigger than the task itself.

That same desire to serve shows up in my free time too. I volunteer as a translator into Spanish for the Ezra Bible App — a tool that helps people read and study Scripture — and I’ve also contributed translations to parts of the XFCE4 desktop environment. I love that open-source work lets me give something back: making good tools accessible to Spanish-speaking users, whether they’re studying the Bible or just trying to use their computer in their own language.

I don’t see my technical work and my faith as separate worlds. Both call me to be faithful, curious, and diligent — to care for what’s been entrusted to me, whether that’s a server, a network, a translation, or a conversation.

What I care about:

  • Doing honest, careful work — even when no one is watching
  • Protecting people’s information as if it were my own
  • Learning something new every day
  • Making technology accessible to more people, in more languages
  • Treating every colleague and user with kindness and respect

A little more about me:

I’m a theologian by training and a Christian by conviction. I believe good technology, like good theology, should serve people well — with clarity, humility, and heart. And sometimes that means translating a Bible app into Spanish, or helping a desktop environment feel like home for someone who speaks your language.
